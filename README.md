# FridgeMate privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

FridgeMate is a kitchen app. It shows what food you have, what to cook, and what to buy. There is no account.

## What stays on your phone

Your kitchen list, shop list, language and settings stay on your device. FridgeMate does not upload that list. A backup is a file you save or open yourself.

## Camera and scans

The camera is used only when you tap Scan.

A photo of food, a barcode or a shopping receipt is sent to xAI so the app can read the products. The photo is used for that reading. It is not sold and it is not used for advertising.

A barcode number is also sent to Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a public food database, to show the product name, ingredients, allergens and additives.

## Payments

FridgeMate Plus is a one-off purchase through Google Play or Amazon. Those stores take the payment. FridgeMate does not receive your card number. The app only remembers on your device that Plus is unlocked.

## What we do not do

We do not create an account, sell your information, or show ads.

## Children

FridgeMate is a household tool. It is not directed at children.

## Removing your information

Use Empty the kitchen in the app menu, or uninstall the app. That removes the list stored on the device.

## Contact

Use the email address on the FridgeMate page in Google Play.
