# FridgeMate — delete your data

This page is for the FridgeMate app.

FridgeMate has no account. Your kitchen is stored on your phone. Use the steps below to request that your data is deleted.

## How to request that your data is deleted

1. Open FridgeMate.
2. Tap the menu button at the top. It is the row of three dots.
3. Tap **Empty the kitchen**.
4. The list on this phone is deleted straight away.

You can also delete everything stored by the app by uninstalling FridgeMate.

## Data that is deleted

- The food in your kitchen, including quantities, where it is kept and use-by dates
- Your shop list
- The language and colour settings saved in the app

Uninstalling FridgeMate deletes the same data, and also removes the on-phone note that FridgeMate Plus is unlocked.

## Data that is kept

A purchase made through Google Play stays in your Google account. FridgeMate cannot delete Google's record of that payment.

FridgeMate does not keep a copy of your kitchen on a server. A scan photo is sent only so that scan can be read, and is not stored afterwards. A barcode number is sent only to look up that product, and the lookup is not kept.

There is no extra retention period. Data on the phone stays until you tap Empty the kitchen or uninstall the app. It is not deleted on a timer.

## Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

FridgeMate shows what food you have, what to cook, and what to buy. There is no account. The kitchen list, shop list, language and settings stay on your device.

The camera is used only when you tap Scan. A photo is sent to xAI so the app can read the products. A barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to show the name, ingredients, allergens and additives. These are not sold and are not used for advertising.

FridgeMate Plus is a one-off purchase through Google Play. FridgeMate does not receive your card number.

FridgeMate is not directed at children.
