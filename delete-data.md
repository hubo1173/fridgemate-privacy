# FridgeMate — delete your data

This page is for the FridgeMate app on Google Play.

FridgeMate has no account. Your kitchen is stored on your phone. Follow the steps below to delete it.

## How to delete your data

1. Open FridgeMate.
2. Tap the menu button at the top. It is the row of three dots.
3. Tap **Empty the kitchen**.
4. The list on this phone is deleted straight away.

You can also delete everything stored by the app by uninstalling FridgeMate.

## What is deleted

Empty the kitchen deletes the data FridgeMate stores on that phone:

- the food in your kitchen, including quantities, where it is kept and use-by dates
- your shop list
- the language and colour settings saved in the app

Uninstalling the app deletes the same data, and also removes the on-phone note that FridgeMate Plus is unlocked. A purchase made through Google Play stays in your Google account. FridgeMate cannot delete Google's record of that payment.

## What FridgeMate does not keep

FridgeMate does not keep a copy of your kitchen on a server.

- A scan photo is sent to xAI only so that scan can be read. FridgeMate does not store the photo afterwards.
- A barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts only to look up that product. FridgeMate does not keep the lookup.

There is no extra retention period. Data on the phone stays until you tap Empty the kitchen or uninstall the app. It is not deleted on a timer.
